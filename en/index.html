PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

The provider of the CharaBen app (the “Developer”) handles user information as described below. This English version is provided for reference; the Japanese version is the original policy.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

CharaBen stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Question sets, questions and answers, learning records, review status, and folder, archive, and pin settings
  • Character settings, character names, display images, expression images, and audio
  • Display preferences, notification settings, and onboarding completion status
  • Information needed to export and restore backups

This information is used to provide learning, display, review, backup, and restore features. It is not collected by or sent to servers operated by the Developer. CharaBen does not require account registration or use its own analytics SDK.

When you export a backup, the backup file is created in the location you choose. If you choose external storage, such as iCloud Drive, the handling of that information is subject to the policies of that service.

2. Advertising

CharaBen displays banner advertising through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC, to support operating costs.

Advertising providers may collect or use the following information to deliver ads, prevent fraud, measure ad impressions, and improve service quality:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from the IP address
  • Device or advertising SDK identifiers
  • Technical information, such as device, OS and app version, and language settings
  • Information about ad impressions, viewing, and interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as crash logs and app launch time

At launch, CharaBen does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission and does not use IDFA-based tracking advertising to track users. However, the information above may be used for ad delivery and measurement depending on the advertising SDK and advertising provider settings.

For Google’s handling of information, please see the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads SDK App Store data disclosure.

Where required by applicable law or regional requirements, the Developer provides consent collection and privacy choices for advertising.

CharaBen also offers an optional non-consumable in-app purchase that permanently removes ads. Apple processes purchases and restores. The Developer does not receive your payment card information, but the app receives the transaction and entitlement information required to provide the purchased feature.

3. Notifications

If you enable learning reminders, CharaBen uses local notifications on your device. Notification content and notification settings are not sent to the Developer’s servers.

4. Photos, Audio, and Files

Files you select as character images, expression images, or audio are stored on your device for character settings and display. These files are not sent to the Developer.

When you import question JSON or a backup file, the selected file is processed on your device to provide the relevant feature.

5. External AI Services

CharaBen can copy a prompt for creating question JSON with external AI services, such as ChatGPT. CharaBen does not directly connect to an external AI service. Whether to enter information into an external AI service is your choice, and information entered there is subject to that service’s policies.

6. Disclosure to Third Parties

Except for the advertising providers described in Section 2, the Developer does not provide your learning content, character settings, images, audio, or backup contents to third parties.

7. Use by Minors

Where parental consent is required by applicable law, CharaBen is intended to be used with the involvement of a parent or guardian. Because the app includes advertising, please review the App Store age rating and device content restrictions.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy or the handling of information, contact charaben.support@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

The Developer may update this policy in response to changes in law, advertising SDK specifications, or CharaBen features. Material changes will be announced on this page or in the app.
